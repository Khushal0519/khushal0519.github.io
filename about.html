My Background

Hands-on security experience, backed by research.

My background combines an MSc in Applied Cyber Security from Queen's University Belfast with a B.Tech in Computer Engineering specialising in Cybersecurity from Vishwakarma University, Pune.

During my time at Bekaert Industries, I gained practical experience in vulnerability assessment and penetration testing across enterprise networks, servers, endpoints and CCTV systems, alongside Active Directory administration and IT infrastructure security. I have also worked on security alert investigation and reporting exercises, analysing suspicious network and endpoint activity and supporting remediation decisions.

My academic research includes studying privilege escalation techniques through Vishwakarma University and Binghamton University collaboration, as well as investigating Android greyware security during my MSc. I continue to expand my cloud security knowledge and build practical labs to turn technical learning into effective security outcomes.
